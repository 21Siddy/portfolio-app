⬡ Kernel (KE) — The Crown Core

Moves 1 square in all 8 directions (like a King). Captures on contact.

Bodyguard Protection Rule:

When attacked (by move or shot), an adjacent friendly guard is sacrificed instead. The Kernel survives and the attacker remains on its origin tile!

Sacrifice Priority: Ping > Exploit > Trace > Daemon > Ice.

▲ Ping (PI) — Recon Probe

Moves orthogonally up to 2 empty squares. Cannot jump.

Captures diagonally up to 2 squares onto an enemy piece (moves onto target).

👾 Daemon (DE) — Background Heavy

Slides orthogonally up to 3 squares. At range 2–3 captures by moving onto target. An adjacent enemy blocks movement.

Shoot Action: Shoots any adjacent enemy (all 8 directions). The Daemon does not move.

⚡ Exploit (EX) — Rogue Subroutine

L-shaped jump (2 + 1 squares, like a Knight). Leaps over all pieces and locked squares.

Captures by landing directly on an enemy piece.

◉ Trace (TR) — Scanner Turret

Moves 1 square orthogonally onto an empty tile.

Sniper Shot: Shoots diagonally at range 2–3. The Trace does not move. Range 1 blocks line and cannot be shot.

❄ Ice (IC) — Defensive Shield

Slides up to 2 squares in any of the 8 directions. Path must be clear.

Captures by moving onto an enemy piece.

🔥 Firewall Shrink & Endgame Resolution

At the end of rounds 8, 16, 24, and 26, the firewall locks the outermost open ring (8x8 → 6x6 → 4x4 → 2x2 → locked). All pieces standing on newly locked squares are immediately destroyed!

Bare Kernels Rule: When only the two Kernels remain, if distance $\le 2$, the team that just moved loses; otherwise the Kernel closest to the center wins. Ties go to Magenta!
